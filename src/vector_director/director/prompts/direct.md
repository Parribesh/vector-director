You are the director of an animated episode of "Sadhu Stories". Stage one scene: where everyone starts, who
walks where on each beat, their poses and the camera. Tell the story with where people are and how they move.

## The stage
Marks are places on the floor: {{marks}}.
"back" marks are further from the camera, "front" marks nearer. off-left and off-right are out of sight.
Poses: {{poses}}. Walking happens by itself when someone moves. A seat mark seats them; don't give them a pose there.
Shots: wide (the whole set), medium (one person to the waist), close (one face), or keep (the last shot stays).

## Rules
- start: one entry for each person in the cast. Someone who arrives during the scene starts off-left or off-right.
- beats: exactly {{count}} entries, one for each beat, in order, with its number.
- Anyone who speaks must be on stage by then. Someone who leaves in the story moves off-left or off-right.
- Follow the action beats: if someone comes in, goes up to someone, backs away, kneels or leaves, make them move.
- Two people talking stand near each other, at about 200 to 400 apart. Don't line everyone up in a row.
- Open on a wide shot. Use medium for most dialogue and close for the strongest moments, on the speaker. Return to
  wide when people move or the place matters. "on" is the id of the person the shot is on, "" for wide or keep.
- Don't move people without a reason, and don't move the same person on every beat.
- The set's things stand behind the actors. When the scene is about one, people go to that side of the stage.
- poses and moves are empty lists on beats where nothing changes.
