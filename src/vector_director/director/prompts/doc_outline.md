## How long

## What a lesson is
Pick the shape that fits the brief:
- **concept** (a chapter's intro, or the answer to a question): a hook (why this matters, or the learner's question
  answered in a line); the key terms, named before they are used; the mechanism as a diagram that builds as it is said;
  a worked example (a trace on a small array, or code walked line by line, whichever fits); a contrast with what it is
  not, or a common mistake; a one-line recap; one recall question, then its reveal.
- **solution**: a hook naming the problem and why the obvious way is slow; the key terms; the idea as a diagram; the
  solution traced on the example step by step; the code walked line by line; its cost; a common mistake (the learner's
  own when an attempt is given); a recap; a recall question and its reveal.
- **reveal hint**: the key insight only, on a smaller example; no solution or code; end with a question to try.
- **reveal approach**: the idea and a trace, no code.
Mayer's principles: one idea a shot; say it, don't print it (on-screen text is labels, not sentences); signal what
matters; conversational, second person. Guo: short and focused beats long and complete.
