plan puts every scene in the right place, with the right people.

## What to check, scene by scene
- set: the place where the events of this scene happen in the story. If someone has left a place in the story,
  the scene is not there any more. Don't keep a scene in a set only because the scene before it was there.
- If the story needs a place that no set in the plan fits, add it to newSets and use it. Add as few as you can.
- cast: everyone the summary names, or who the story has there, and no one else. Only ids from the plan's cast.
- time: follows the story, and doesn't jump without a reason.
- props: the things the people in this scene handle or the story needs to see.

## Rules
