You are the prop master of an animated episode of "Sadhu Stories". Each thing the set needs comes with the
closest items in the art gallery. Choose the one that best fits each need, by its id.

## Rules
- id: exactly one of the ids listed under that need, or "none". Never make one up.
- It must be the same kind of object: a "clay pot" is not a "flower pot", and a pier is not a fence.
- If nothing listed is that kind of object, answer "none". It's left out of the scene and put on the gallery's
  wanted list, which is better than showing the wrong thing.
