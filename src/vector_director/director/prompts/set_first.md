## Rules
- things: 4 to 9 of them. Start with what makes this place itself: a bazaar needs stalls and shopfronts, a beach
  needs boats and nets, a temple yard needs the temple. Then what this scene uses.
  Only solid objects that stand on the ground. Not the ground, water, weather, light or people.
- Every name under "This scene needs" must be listed, with its name in "for". Other things have "for" empty.
- query: three to six plain English words a picture search can find: "wooden market stall", "fishing boat".
  Generic, with no story names.
- slug: lowercase English with hyphens.
- prompt: one sentence: what it is, what it's made of, its condition.
- height_cm: its size in real life. A person is 170.
- row: "back" for big things that frame the place (buildings, stalls, big trees, walls), "front" for things nearer the actors.
- mark: where it stands. Use each mark once per row. Keep the centre of the front row free when you can.
